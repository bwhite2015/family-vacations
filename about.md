Hiya! Ben here. I made this site as a way of keeping a digital scrapbook of various trips and vacations that my family and I go on. It's also a good way to keep relatives updated and following along as they desire. 

Feel free to view past [trips]({{ '/trips/' | relative_url }}), read [blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}) entries, and explore visited locations on the [map]({{ '/map/' | relative_url }}). Each trip consists of one or more blog entries and each blog entry contains tagged locations that appear on our growing map of adventures! You can also simply scroll thru a list of [places]({{ '/locations/' | relative_url }}) we've visited too. 

Hope you get as much out of these adventures as we have!
