This is where we keep the write-ups and photos from our family trips — partly so
we remember them, partly so the grandparents can follow along.

Entries are tagged by place and by vacation, so you can pull up everything from
a single trip, or everything we've ever written about one spot. The
[map]({{ '/map/' | relative_url }}) has a pin for every place we've tagged.

Feel free to poke around.
