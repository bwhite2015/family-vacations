Honeymoon - Day 8 - 8/25/24

---

Today, we drove 9 hours home from our honeymoon. The final day. I got us home in one piece. I'll continue my recitation below:

On Day 5, Aubri and I went to {% include loc.html id="up-marquette" %}. Our first stop was {% include loc.html id="up-sugarloaf" %}, a very short but uphill hike that left us somewhat winded but awestruck at the 360 degree view of the surrounding land. Stretching from East to West, the coastline of Northern UP butted up right against {% include loc.html id="up-lake-superior" %} and brought the forests to a halt. A remarkable sight to be sure. 

![The summit of Sugarloaf Mountain with Marquette in the distance.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/up-honeymoon-2024/IMG_20240822_133239.jpg)

Once we got into Downtown Marquette proper we walked the strip of local shops down to a pub called {% include loc.html id="up-iron-bay-tap-room" %}. Here we got a tasty meal of a pulled pork sandwich and a burger with bacon and goat cheese. These were both great, however the sides of pickled veggies with hummus overstayed their welcome for me. I also got the Saison Terre A Terre Farmhouse Ale by Barrel and Beam. It was decent, though, a small portion, which was fine since I was still trying to recover my microbiome in my stomach. 

![Downtown Marquette selfie in front of the Iron Bay Tap Room.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/up-honeymoon-2024/IMG_20240822_153215.jpg)

After this, exiting The Iron Bay Tap Room, I looked up to see {% include loc.html id="up-iron-ore-dock" %}, although I didn't know what it was until I bothered to ask an employee in the nearby establishment, {% include loc.html id="up-thill-sons" %}. Before leaving Marquette, we'd purchase 2 pounds of whitefish for $24. For now, we'd hold off until we could get some ice for them. We checked out some shops and got ice cream at {% include loc.html id="up-yoop-phoria" %} before walking down to the local sports store to get an idea of potential climbing locations around the area. Along the way, we checked out the local bookstore, also on {% include loc.html id="up-3rd-st-marquette" %}, {% include loc.html id="up-snowbound-books" %}. This victorian home was converted into a grand library of purchasable works. We didn't purchase any since I was still working on Walkter Isaacson's Benjamin Franklin. 

![The Iron Ore Dock next to Thill & Sons Fish House.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/up-honeymoon-2024/IMG_20240822_153457.jpg)

Once we got to the local sporting goods store, called {% include loc.html id="up-downwind-marquette" %}, We asked a man named Bill what he knew about local crags, which ended up being a lot. Bill gave us directions to {% include loc.html id="up-presque-isle" %} where a local boulder problem called {% include loc.html id="up-traverse" %} sat at cliff's edge facing Lake Superior just 10 feet away from the granite drop off. I worked the problem for a few hours while Aubri got some shots. Once satisfied with my progress, we picked up the fish, drove home and cooked em' up!

![Attempting The Traverse at Presque Isle.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/up-honeymoon-2024/climbing_stillframe_001.jpg)
