We left the motel before six, which felt heroic until we realized nothing opens
before seven and we spent the first hour of the trip sitting in a parking lot
eating gas station donuts.

Worth it, though. The drive into {% include loc.html id="zion" %} at sunrise is
the kind of thing that makes everyone in the car go quiet. Even the kids, which
has never happened before and may never happen again.

## The hike

We did the easy river walk rather than anything ambitious. Ellie found a lizard
and named it Gerald. Gerald was released back into the wild approximately four
minutes later, over significant objection.

By early afternoon we were back on the road toward {% include loc.html id="moab" %},
which is where the flat tire comes in. Somewhere on a very straight, very empty
stretch of highway, we picked up a nail.

> The spare was, of course, at the bottom of everything we owned.

Forty-five minutes and one YouTube tutorial later, we were moving again. We
rolled into town late, ate green chile cheeseburgers, and nobody complained
about anything.

Tomorrow: {% include loc.html id="arches" text="the arches" %}, assuming the
tire holds.
