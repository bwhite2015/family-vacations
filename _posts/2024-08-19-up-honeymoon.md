Honeymoon - Day 2 - 8/19/24

---

Wedding went GREAT on Sat! No issues. Was great to see family and friends cut loose. Groomsmen loved their gifts. Vows were all good. During private vows we got some rain but not critical. Pastor Kevin did a great job as officiant. After the ceremony, we got a bit more rain but then a double rainbow. Great photos! The Groomsmen and Bridesmaids speeches were touching as well (thanks to Noah, Milan, May May, and Hailey). The night of partying was fun, absurd and constant. Everyone got along well and Caleb Hitchcock nailed each caricature he did. 

![Aubri and I catching a rainbow on our wedding day.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/up-honeymoon-2024/IMG_20240818_120821.jpg)

Now, yesterday; Day 1 of Honeymoon. Mostly comprised of travel from the {% include loc.html id="bloom-bower-oh" %} airbnb in {% include loc.html id="risingsun-oh" %} to our home in {% include loc.html id="pemberville-oh" %} to pick up the dogs and some of our luggage, to travel to our first UP hideaway in {% include loc.html id="up-brimley" %}, MI. Aubri got a sneak peak at {% include loc.html id="up-mackinac-island" %} when we went over the {% include loc.html id="up-mackinac-bridge" %} into the UP. We ate that night at {% include loc.html id="up-bay-mills" %} in the casino nearby. The wings I had were very good and Aubri said her fish was decent as well. The dogs settled in and we got to bed around 9:30ish. 

![The view of the Mackinac Bridge as we traveled northbound into the Upper Peninsula.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/up-honeymoon-2024/IMG_20240818_173241.jpg)

Today, we had a lot to do. First, we went to {% include loc.html id="up-tahquamenon-falls" %} with the dogs to walk them. The large falls were tea colored from apparent leaf deposits up stream. Tilly was unimpressed but Boone seemed overwhelmed and unnerved by the Falls and crowds of tourists viewing it. I had to carry her down and up the lengthy flights of stairs due to the grating being too far apart for her feet on each step. She wouldn't even attempt the ascent and descent. 

![Aubri, Tilly and I (Boone is out of view), posing in front of the tea colored Tahquamenon Falls.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/up-honeymoon-2024/IMG_20240819_115859.jpg)

After Tahquamenon we went North to {% include loc.html id="up-whitefish-point" %} to check out the shipwreck museum and lighthouse. We still had the dogs with us at this point so we stopped at the beach first so they could stretch their legs. Boone was intrigued by the water, trying to drink from Lake Superior but was too startled by the cold waves washing up to her paws to stick around for long. In the museum exhibits themselves, I picked Boone up while Aub picked Tilly up just to ensure their cooperation. In the main museum building we saw a brief overview of the history of the lighthouse and its associated shipwrecks. Old scuba suits and scale models scattered the perimeter while the center of the room housed a huge lighthouse 'bulb' with a wax lighthouse attendant underneath tending to its needs. Another museum building, the lighthouse families living space, showed these same wax figures in various positions to illustrate common tasks, chores and pastimes of the families that lived in these rooms. 

Southwest of the museum property is the bird watcher's trail that was a great getaway for the dogs from the riff raff of other visitors on premises. A giant chain led the way up the wood plank trail and, at the tip, an "eagle's nest" for bird watchers to perch and view seasonal fowl. A sign read "Raptors" indicating the area was a common rest stop for migrating eagles, hawks, and other birds of prey. We hurried Tilly back down the path to safety. 

![Aubri and I at Whitefish Point's Shipwreck Museum and Lighthouse.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/up-honeymoon-2024/IMG_20240819_135145.jpg)

We eventually made our way back to our headquarters in Brimley where we dropped off the dogs and continued on to  {% include loc.html id="up-sault-saint-marie" %} to the East. Here, we got the chance to view the freighters pulling into the  {% include loc.html id="up-soo-locks" %}. This lengthy process, detailed in the nearby visitors center, played out as expected with the water levels between the locks lowering from Lake Superior levels to Lake Huron levels to allow the ship to pass through without issue. We only glimpsed the end of one cycle and the beginning of another from the viewing deck but also caught glimpses of the process while walking from shop to shop on the long stretch of storefronts that face the locks. I snagged a chocolate frog from a local candy store and Aub got a chocolate milkshake to sip as we walked along the locks. We eventually went back home to go to bed since we had already eaten prior to journeying to Sault Saint Marie at {% include loc.html id="up-cozy-inn" %} in Brimley. I ate whitefish with mash potatoes and coleslaw. Aubri had the same sides with perch. Both equally delicious! Anyways… Sleep!!

![Aubri watching a small boat pass through the Soo Locks into Lake Huron's side.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/up-honeymoon-2024/IMG_20240819_174945.jpg)
