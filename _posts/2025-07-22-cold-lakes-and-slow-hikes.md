A slower week than we planned, and better for it.

We based ourselves in {% include loc.html id="estes-park" %} and mostly let the
days happen. The lake near the cabin never got above about fifty degrees, which
did not stop anyone under twelve from getting in it daily.

## The world's slowest hike

Two and a half miles took us four hours. In fairness, we stopped for: nine rocks
worth examining, one marmot, a snack, a second snack, an extended debate about
whether marmots hibernate, and a nap in a meadow.

I have no notes. It was perfect.

We drove down through {% include loc.html id="santa-fe" %} on the way home and
stretched the trip out an extra day, which turned out to be the right call —
the light there in the evening is something else.
